## Manual QA (2026-09-24, polish round after QA)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack in
  `tools/passages_src.json` (`"src": "gen"`). `python3 -m packbuilder passages .`
  writes `pack/passages.json` and this report. Two runs give byte-identical
  output.
- **Linking.** Sentences are linked first by `core.sentences.sentence_links`,
  the code the Tatoeba sentences use. A fallback then links any counted token
  whose lemma is a pack word under another part of speech: molto and tutto as
  determiners, solo, comune, lontano as an adjective, po', mezza and dolce.
  Compound heads do not link, so "rispetto a" and the "fine" of "fine
  settimana" stay unlinked while "settimana" links.
- **Unlinked tokens.** Before this round, many in-pack surfaces did not link:
  molto 26/53, tutto 21/49, po' 10/10, comune 8/9, mezza 6/6, solo 6/20,
  lontano 3/6 and dolce 1/1. Now 4 of 6221 in-pack tokens are unlinked, and
  all 4 are the compound heads above (fine 3, rispetto 1).

  lowercase token the tagger calls PROPN now counts. Sentence-initial names
  that the truecaser lowers (Bruno, Luisa) are declared in `oop` with the
