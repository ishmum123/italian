### Residual classes (not fixed)

1. **Sense ranking when the head English word is shared.** When the gloss
   words that separate two senses are rare in the translations, the
   association score ties or picks the sense whose head word ("be", "make",

## Deviations from the brief

- The spaCy model licence is **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0**, not MIT. spaCy itself is
  MIT; `it_core_news_sm` is trained on UD Italian ISDT. It is used at build
  time only. The README and attribution.json record this.
- Surface counts are **split proportionally** over the (lemma, POS) readings
  seen in context, with readings under 2.5% dropped (5% in v2). A pure majority vote would
  lose sei = "six" and letto = "bed" again.
- UD and Wiktionary disagree on POS for many closed-class words (altro,
  stesso and tale are ADJ in UD but det in Wiktionary; però and tuttavia are
  ADV in UD but conj). The POS map therefore has ordered fallbacks. The `pos`
  field still shows the corpus POS.
- **Loanwords** are excluded only when they are English borrowings, in the
  English top 2000 and seen fewer than 10 times in the Italian corpus. The
  brief's rule alone would drop il film, lo sport and l'hotel.
- **Passato remoto** is detected on AUX as well as VERB (fu, ebbe), plus
  Wiktionary "historic past" forms the tagger misses.
- Clitics mi/ti map to io/tu through a two-entry table, because spaCy
  lemmatises ti as si.
- **Word ids:** ids of unchanged (lemma, pos) pairs are reused from v1 through
  the frozen `tools/id_map_v1.json`. That covers 1,676 words. Changed or new
  words get ids above w2000, so learner progress on correct v1 items survives.
- `primary` sentence assignment is kept out of the pack, as the brief asks.
  The "primary word level" line in the Sentences section summarises it.
